## 5. Deep-research design and free-service evidence (2026-11-11 pass, for doc 13 v3)
- **R-58 [V] Anthropic engineering, "How we built our multi-agent research system"** (`anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system`): orchestrator-worker; subagents in parallel with their own context windows; a separate citation agent; effort scaling in prompts (simple fact-finding 1 agent with 3-10 tool calls; direct comparisons 2-4 subagents with 10-15 calls each; complex research more than 10 subagents); parallelism cut time by up to 90% on complex queries; start with short broad queries then narrow; agents about 4x and multi-agent about 15x the tokens of chat; failure modes (50 subagents for simple queries, endless search for nonexistent sources, SEO farms ranked above primary sources, duplicated work from vague tasks); resume from checkpoints.
- **R-59 [V] LangChain, "Open Deep Research"** (`langchain.com/blog/open-deep-research`): scope, research brief, supervisor with parallel researchers in isolated contexts, one-shot report writing; writing sections in parallel gave disjoint reports; no numeric evaluation published.
- **R-60 [S] GPT Researcher** (README and write-ups seen in search results): a planner generates questions; one crawler agent per question runs in parallel; each source summarized and tracked; a publisher aggregates. Run-time and cost figures from a single blog are unverified.
- **R-61 [S] Gemini Deep Research** (third-party guides of the Interactions API): collaborative planning returns a plan to review before running; streaming with thought summaries; background execution; public preview, paid tiers.
- **R-62 [S, excerpts] "Don't Stop Early" (ACL industry 2026, arXiv 2604.24978), ADORE (arXiv 2601.18267), VeriTrace (arXiv 2605.26081):** coverage-driven objectives, evidence-based sufficiency criteria, coverage audits with targeted follow-up retrieval, stopping on coverage and stability; removing termination criteria hurt quality most.
